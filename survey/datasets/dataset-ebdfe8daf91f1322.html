Open dataset ↗

A 3D-grounded embodied world model (EWM) benchmark built on RoboTwin 2.0 covering 50 manipulation tasks across four regimes with 5,000 episodes and 25,000 multi-view ground-truth videos. · Source

Sensors
Simulated camera (5 synchronized views) via RoboTwin 2.0 simulator

Dimensions

  • 5,000 ground-truth episodes (100 per task) · full dataset, 50 tasks · Source
  • 25,000 ground-truth videos (5 camera views per episode); 145,000 total videos scored in evaluation (5,000 ground-truth, 20,000 reconstructed, 120,000 generated) · full dataset · Source
  • 5 time-synchronized camera views per episode · all episodes · Source
  • 50 tasks across 4 regimes · full dataset · Source

Data types

  • synthetic multi-view RGB video (ground-truth) · Source
  • view-aware natural-language text conditions (3 per instance) · Source
