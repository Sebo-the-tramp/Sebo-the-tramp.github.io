Open dataset ↗

A benchmark for image-based 3D reconstruction with sequences acquired in realistic outdoor and indoor conditions, ground-truth captured using an industrial laser scanner, and high-resolution video sequences provided as input. · Source

Download size
4K video · raw input video sequences per scene (advanced users download the raw 4K videos captured with a high-end camera)
Origin
Real
Sensors
industrial laser scanner (for ground-truth), Sony camera (for several scenes), DJI (for several scenes)

Dimensions

  • 14 scenes in two groups (intermediate and advanced) · test set · Source
  • 4K (high-resolution video) · raw video per scene; exact pixel dimensions not stated on the page · Source

Data types

  • real (acquired outside the lab, in realistic conditions) · Source
  • RGB video · Source
  • ground-truth point clouds (scanned with industrial laser scanner) · Source
  • training image sets (frames sampled from 29.97 fps video) with ground-truth geometry · Source
