Open dataset ↗

SceneSplat-7K is a collection of 7,916 indoor 3D Gaussian Splatting scenes optimized from eight established 3D datasets, used to train the SceneSplat feed-forward open-vocabulary 3DGS scene encoder. · Source

Download size
7,916 scenes; 11.27 billion 3D Gaussian splats · full release across all eight source datasets
Sensors
Real indoor RGB-D captures (ScanNet, ScanNet++, Replica, ARKitScenes, Matterport3D, 3RScan) and synthetic renders (Hypersim), aggregated into 3DGS
