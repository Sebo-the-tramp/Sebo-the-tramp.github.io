Open dataset ↗

A multi-view stereo evaluation dataset of small object scenes captured by a camera on an industrial robot arm from 49 or 64 calibrated positions under seven lighting conditions, with structured-light reference geometry. · Source

Listed size
6.3 GB · SampleSet zip (ReadMe, calibration data, camera projection matrices, observability masks, evaluation code, and all data for scans set1 and set6)
