Open dataset ↗

Virtual KITTI 2 is a photo-realistic synthetic video dataset created with the Unity engine to replicate five real KITTI driving sequences with stereo cameras and fully rendered ground truth for detection, tracking, segmentation, depth and flow tasks. · Source
