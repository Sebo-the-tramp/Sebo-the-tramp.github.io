Open dataset ↗

A 3D inpainting benchmark of 20 diverse real-world scenes (indoor to outdoor) with wide view variations and complex interfacial surfaces, released alongside IMFine (CVPR 2025). · Source

Sensors
DJI Pocket 3 (60fps capture)

Dimensions

  • 1920 × 1080 px · capture resolution per scene (experiments use half resolution) · Source
  • 20 scenes · full dataset (50 eval images + 125 training images per scene) · Source

Data types

  • real multi-view RGB images (175 per scene, captured at 60fps) · Source
  • camera poses · Source
  • object masks (with inpainting masks derived from them) · Source
  • Gaussian Splatting scenes reconstructed from object-present views · Source
