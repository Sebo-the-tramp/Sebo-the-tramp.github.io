Open dataset ↗

A large-scale LiDAR-visual dataset with millimetre-accurate TLS ground truth for benchmarking localisation, 3D reconstruction, and novel-view synthesis around Oxford landmarks. · Source

Listed size
1300.0 GB · full source Hugging Face repository: 24 sequences with raw images, raw LiDAR sweeps, ROS 1/ROS 2 bags, VILENS-SLAM and COLMAP outputs, per-site TLS reference maps (~76 GB), and benchmark artifacts
Download size
~1.3 TB · full source Hugging Face repository: 24 sequences with raw images, raw LiDAR sweeps, ROS 1/ROS 2 bags, VILENS-SLAM and COLMAP outputs, per-site TLS reference maps (~76 GB), and benchmark artifacts
Sensors
LiDAR — Hesai QT64 (64 channels, 10 Hz, 60 m range, ±3 cm accuracy), RGB cameras ×3 — Alphasense Core (global shutter, colour fisheye, 20 Hz, 1440×1080), IMU — Alphasense Core (MEMS, 400 Hz, FPGA-synchronised), Terrestrial LiDAR scanner — Leica RTC360 (millimetre-accurate ground-truth map)

Dimensions

  • 1440 × 1080 px · each of the three global-shutter colour fisheye cameras · Source
  • 24 sequences · full dataset, recorded across six Oxford sites · Source
  • >125,000 m² · total area covered across the six sites (average distance per sequence >400 m) · Source

Data types

  • real (physically captured, not synthetic) · Source
  • RGB (colour fisheye images, cam0–cam2) · Source
  • sparse LiDAR point clouds (Hesai QT64, 10 Hz, 60 m range) · Source
  • dense TLS ground-truth point cloud maps (Leica RTC360) · Source
  • IMU telemetry (400 Hz, hardware-synchronised) · Source
  • centimetre-accurate ground-truth trajectories (LiDAR-to-TLS registered) · Source
