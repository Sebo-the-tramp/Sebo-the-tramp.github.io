Open dataset ↗

MVS-Synth is a photo-realistic synthetic dataset of urban scenes captured in Grand Theft Auto V, with 120 sequences of 100 frames each, providing RGB images, ground-truth depth maps, and camera parameters for learning-based Multi-View Stereo. · Source
