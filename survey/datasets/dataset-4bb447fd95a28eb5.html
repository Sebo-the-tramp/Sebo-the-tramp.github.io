Open dataset ↗

AnyVisLoc is a large-scale low-altitude multi-view UAV dataset with 20,077 DJI images from 24 scenes across China, paired with 2.5D aerial and satellite reference maps, released for the UAV absolute visual localization benchmark (CVPR 2026 Findings). · Source
