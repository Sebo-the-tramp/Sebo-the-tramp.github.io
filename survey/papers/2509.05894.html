Abstract

Given a continuous piecewise linear function $f:\mathbb{R}^{n_0} \to \mathbb{R}$ and a fixed architecture of feedforward ReLU neural networks, the exact function realization problem asks when a network of that architecture realizes $f$. In contrast, expressivity concerns characterizing all functions exactly realizable by a given architecture. To address these questions systematically, we establish a connection between toric geometry and ReLU neural networks, allowing tools from algebraic geometry to analyze the structures. Starting with an unbiased ReLU network with rational weights, we define the ReLU fan, the ReLU toric variety, and the ReLU Cartier divisor associated with the network. This work also relates the tropical and the toric geometry: the former captures combinatorial data of ReLU networks, while the latter recovers the underlying geometric structure. As an application, we obtain a complete characterization of the functions realizable by unbiased shallow ReLU networks with rational weights. The characterization is expressed in terms of intersection numbers of ReLU Cartier divisors with torus-invariant curves and leads to a decomposition of piecewise-linear functions into shallow and non-shallow components.
